        About these numbers

        Lyrics for every song on each rapper’s studio albums, as listed on MusicBrainz, from the open lyrics database LRCLIB. Mixtapes and group albums aren’t included, and guest verses on a rapper’s own albums count toward them. Only rappers with lyrics for at least 40 songs are ranked.

        The numbers are songs, not mentions: a song that says “Maybach” twenty times counts once. Slang got read by hand and left out where it means something else. A “chicken” that costs a verse is not dinner, Killa Bees are not bees, a crown is not Crown Royal, and Gucci Mane saying his own name is not a Gucci mention.
