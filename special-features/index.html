Who’s the most-featured rapper? Depends how you count.

      About these numbers

      Counts come from each rapper’s English Wikipedia discography: the “as featured artist” singles plus the non-single guest appearances, with a song listed in both counted once. Snoop Dogg has his own full list of guest appearances on Wikipedia, so his count is the most complete here. Sixteen rappers with no Wikipedia list (Mac Dre, Chuck D, El-P and others) are counted from MusicBrainz guest credits instead, with no chart data.

      Biggest features scores every featured single that made the Billboard Hot 100: a No. 1 is worth 100 points, a No. 100 is worth 1. A remix verse gets no credit for the original song’s peak. Hit rate only counts rappers with at least 10 featured singles. Group members whose features are credited to the group, like Salt-N-Pepa or Run-DMC, aren’t ranked on their own.
