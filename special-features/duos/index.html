Rap’s real duos.

Not groups. The pairs who keep turning up on each other’s records, counted song by song.

    How duos are counted

    Every song two rappers share, taken from both of their Wikipedia discographies: featured singles and guest appearances, each song counted once. Members of the same group (Hot Boys, UGK, Dipset, the Wu, Bone Thugs and so on) aren’t counted as duos with each other. A rapper’s own albums aren’t in the data, so a duo made of one rapper’s solo tracks with the other on them shows up through the guest’s side.
