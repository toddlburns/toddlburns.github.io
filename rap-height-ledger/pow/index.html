About these heights

Every height here is a tall tale until proven otherwise. They are as reported, not measured: shoes, posture and the odd generous lyric all play a part, so read every figure as ± an inch or two. When a rapper has said their own height on record, that’s the figure here. Long-running arguments (Drake, 2Pac, Biggie, RZA) are marked disputed, and heights that only height-tracking sites list are marked unconfirmed.

* Athletes who put out rap records (Shaq, Kobe, Iverson and co.) wear pinstripes and an asterisk, and are left out of the game. Portraits are halftones made from freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons and Flickr; each photographer is credited on the rapper’s card. City is roughly where they rep; era is set by debut year. Spotted a wrong one? Let us know.
