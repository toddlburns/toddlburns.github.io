Records

Rappers only; athletes don’t count.

Every height here is a tall tale until proven otherwise. They are as reported, not measured: shoes, posture and the odd generous lyric all play a part, so read every figure as ± an inch or two. Figures marked Wikidata come from Wikidata; the rest are the commonly reported ones. Long-running arguments (Drake, 2Pac, Biggie, Lil Wayne, RZA) are marked disputed.

* Athletes who put out rap records (Shaq, Kobe, Iverson and co.) wear pinstripes and an asterisk, and are left out of the records. Portraits are halftones made from freely licensed photos on Wikimedia Commons; each photographer is credited on the rapper’s card. City is roughly where they rep; era is set by debut year.
