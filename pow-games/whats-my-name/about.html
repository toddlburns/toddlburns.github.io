What’s My Name?

Name the rapper from the voice alone. Five MCs a day, the same five for everyone.

How it works

Each MC has ten pads, and each pad plays an 8-second verse. The first pad is free. The rest cost 1 to 3 points, and the easier the clip, the more it costs.

Every MC starts at 20 points, and each pad you hit comes off that. You get one guess. Right, and you win what’s left. Wrong, or a pass, and you get zero.

The clips

Every clip starts mid-verse, never on the hook, and never says the MC’s name. The free pads are deep cuts; the expensive ones are the hits.

Drawn from the corners of rap Passion of the Weiss has covered since 2005.
