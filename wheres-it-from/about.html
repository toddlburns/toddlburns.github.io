Where's It From?

A piece of music plays. You drop a pin where you think it was made. Five rounds, up to 5,000 points each.

Scoring

Within 25 km is a perfect 5,000. Beyond that, points fall off with distance: about 2,000 at 550 km, and close to nothing once you're on the wrong continent.

What's in it

94 places in 47 countries, from Detroit techno and Croydon dubstep to Ethiopian jazz, Peruvian chicha, Anatolian rock, Thai molam and Balinese gamelan. Everything is instrumental (or close to it), so there are no lyrics to give the language away. The place is where the music was made, or the scene it belongs to.

The first rounds lean towards sounds with famous homes. The last ones go deeper.
