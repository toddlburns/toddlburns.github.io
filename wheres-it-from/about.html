Where's It From?

A piece of music plays. You drop a pin where you think it was made. Five rounds. Your score is the total number of miles you were off. Lowest wins.

What's in it

166 places in 83 countries, from Detroit techno and Croydon dubstep to Ethiopian jazz, Peruvian chicha, Anatolian rock, Thai molam, Burundian drums, Paraguayan harp and Balinese gamelan. Everything is instrumental (or close to it), so there are no lyrics to give the language away. The place is where the music was made, or the scene it belongs to.

The first rounds lean towards sounds with famous homes. The last ones go deeper.
