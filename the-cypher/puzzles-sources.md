# The Cypher — sources

One line per group: why each tile fits. **VERIFY** marks a group I'm not fully sure of. Levels: 1 easiest, 4 hardest.

## #1
- L1 Black Hippy, Clocked In: Kendrick Lamar, ScHoolboy Q, Ab-Soul, Jay Rock are the four members of TDE's Black Hippy.
- L2 Odd Future, Golf Wang Division: Tyler, Earl Sweatshirt, Domo Genesis, Hodgy were all Odd Future members. Vince Staples was OF-adjacent but not a member.
- L3 Project Blowed Alumni: Aceyalone and Myka 9 (Freestyle Fellowship), Abstract Rude and Busdriver all came up at Project Blowed in Leimert Park.
- L4 Long Beach, Not Snoop: Vince Staples (North Long Beach), Warren G, Nate Dogg and RBX (Snoop's cousin) are all from Long Beach.

## #2
- L1 Geto Boys, All Eras: Scarface, Willie D, Bushwick Bill, and Big Mike (who replaced Willie D on *Till Death Do Us Part*, 1993).
- L2 Screwed Up Click: Fat Pat, Big Hawk, Lil Keke, Big Moe all recorded with DJ Screw as SUC members.
- L3 Came Up on Swishahouse: Mike Jones, Paul Wall, Chamillionaire and Archie Lee all broke through on Swishahouse (Northside Houston) tapes and releases. **VERIFY** (Archie Lee).
- L4 Slab Spec Sheet: Swangas (wire wheels that poke out), candy paint, the fifth wheel (trunk-mounted spare) and the popped trunk are the parts of a Houston slab.

## #3
- L1 Hieroglyphics: Del, Casual, Pep Love, and producer Domino are all Hieroglyphics members.
- L2 The Click: E-40 with his brother D-Shot, sister Suga-T and cousin B-Legit, all on Sick Wid It.
- L3 Say It Like You're From Oakland: hella, hyphy, scraper (a jacked-up American sedan) and ghost ride (the whip) are all Bay Area slang.
- L4 The Pack: Lil B, Young L, Stunna Bam and Lil Uno made up the Berkeley group behind "Vans" (2006).

## #4
- L1 D12: Eminem, Proof, Bizarre and Kuniva were all D12 members.
- L2 Slum Village, Any Lineup: J Dilla, T3 and Baatin founded it; Elzhi joined after Dilla left.
- L3 Marshall's Other Names: Slim Shady (alter ego), Ken Kaniff (a skit character), M&M (his early stage name), B-Rabbit (his *8 Mile* character).
- L4 Bruiser Brigade: Danny Brown's Detroit crew with ZelooperZ, Bruiser Wolf and Dopehead. **VERIFY** (Dopehead).

## #5
- L1 Griselda: Westside Gunn and Conway (founders), Benny the Butcher, and Armani Caesar (signed 2020).
- L2 Def Jux: El-P (co-founder), Aesop Rock (*Labor Days*), Cannibal Ox (*The Cold Vein*) and Mr. Lif (*I Phantom*) all released on Definitive Jux.
- L3 Every Beat by Madlib: *Madvillainy* (with DOOM), *Piñata* and *Bandana* (with Freddie Gibbs), *Sound Ancestors* (his own).
- L4 Every Beat by The Alchemist: *Alfredo* (Freddie Gibbs), *Haram* (Armand Hammer), *Return of the Mac* (Prodigy), *Covert Coup* (Curren$y).

## #6
- L1 Three 6 Mafia: Juicy J, DJ Paul, Lord Infamous and Gangsta Boo were all members.
- L2 Hypnotize Minds, Not in the Group: Project Pat, La Chat, Lil Wyte and Frayser Boy all released on Three 6's Hypnotize Minds label without being in the group.
- L3 Yo Gotti's CMG: Moneybagg Yo, 42 Dugg, EST Gee and GloRilla are all signed to Collective Music Group.
- L4 Paper Route Empire: Young Dolph (founder), Key Glock, Kenny Muney, Big Moochie Grape. **VERIFY** (Big Moochie Grape).

## #7
- L1 Hot Boys: Lil Wayne, Juvenile, B.G., Turk.
- L2 No Limit Soldiers: Master P, Silkk the Shocker, C-Murder (all brothers) and Mystikal, all signed to No Limit.
- L3 Bounce Lifers: Big Freedia, Katey Red, Cheeky Blakk and DJ Jubilee are New Orleans bounce artists, none of them Hot Boys or No Limit.
- L4 Mannie Fresh Made the Beats: *400 Degreez* (Juvenile), *Tha Block Is Hot* (Wayne), *Chopper City in the Ghetto* (B.G.), *Guerrilla Warfare* (Hot Boys), all produced by Mannie Fresh.

## #8
- L1 Wu-Tang, Minus the Obvious Star: RZA, GZA, Raekwon, Masta Killa (Method Man is left out because *Tical* came out in 1994).
- L2 N.W.A, No Cube: Eazy-E, MC Ren, DJ Yella, Dr. Dre.
- L3 Debuted the Same Year as Illmatic (1994): OutKast (*Southernplayalisticadillacmuzik*), Warren G (*Regulate… G Funk Era*), Da Brat (*Funkdafied*), Bone Thugs-n-Harmony (*Creepin on ah Come Up*).
- L4 Real Name Is Christopher: Christopher Wallace, Christopher Rios, Christopher Bridges, Christopher Reid.

## #9
- L1 The Pharcyde: Fatlip, Slimkid3, Imani, Bootie Brown.
- L2 Out on Stones Throw: *Madvillainy*, *Donuts* (J Dilla), *The Unseen* (Quasimoto), *Champion Sound* (Jaylib).
- L3 Brainfeeder: Flying Lotus (founder), Thundercat, Teebs, Lapalux.
- L4 Dilla Was Behind the Boards: "Runnin'" (Pharcyde), "The Light" (Common), "Stakes Is High" (De La Soul), "Didn't Cha Know" (Erykah Badu).

## #10
- L1 Death Row, Doggystyle Era: Snoop Dogg, Kurupt, Daz Dillinger, Nate Dogg.
- L2 Compton, Not Kendrick: MC Eiht, DJ Quik, The Game, YG.
- L3 Mustard on the Beat: "Rack City" (Tyga), "I'm Different" (2 Chainz), "Paranoid" (Ty Dolla $ign), "Not Like Us" (Kendrick Lamar).
- L4 West Coast Diss Records: "No Vaseline" (Cube at N.W.A), "Hit 'Em Up" (2Pac at Bad Boy), "Dollaz + Sense" (Quik at MC Eiht), "Real Muthaphuckkin G's" (Eazy at Dre and Snoop).

## #11

- L3 Madlib, Other Rooms: Quasimoto, Yesterdays New Quintet, DJ Rels, Beat Konducta.
- L4 Kool Keith, Other Personalities: Dr. Octagon, Black Elvis, Dr. Dooom, Mr. Gerbik. **VERIFY** (Mr. Gerbik).

## #12

- L4 Doomtree, Not P.O.S: Dessa, Sims, Cecil Otter, Mike Mictlan. (P.O.S is left out because he also released on Rhymesayers.)

## #14
- L1 Shoreline Mafia, Original Lineup: OhGeesy, Fenix Flexin, Rob Vicious, Master Kato. **VERIFY** (Master Kato).
- L2 Birth Certificate Says Sean: Shawn Carter, Sean Combs, Sean Anderson, Sean Price.

- L4 Name's a Number: 03 Greedo, E-40, 42 Dugg, Mack 10.
