How it works

  1. Sixteen tiles: rappers, producers, albums, labels, crews, places. They make four hidden groups of four.
  2. Pick four tiles and hit Submit. Get it right and the group locks in with its name.
  3. Get it wrong and you lose a life. You have four. If three of your four belong together, we'll tell you: One away.
  4. Shuffle when the board starts lying to you. It will. Some tiles look like they fit two groups. Each one fits exactly one.

The four levels

1 · Solid · Easy
2 · Stripes · Medium
3 · Dots · Hard
4 · Crosshatch · Heads only

Level 4 rewards the people who read liner notes: producer credits, deep cuts, regional knowledge. Every level has a number and a pattern as well as a color, so you can tell them apart without the color.

Every day

A new puzzle drops daily at midnight Pacific. Everyone gets the same one. Your streak only counts on the day's puzzle. The archive is for practice.

When you finish, every tile links to the Passion of the Weiss archive. The game is the warm-up; the reading is the point.

Credit
