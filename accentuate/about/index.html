About Accentuate

Everyone has an accent. It’s the sound of where you grew up, who raised you, the friends you had at fourteen and the work you do now.

Accents are some of the most local things we carry around, and some of the most easily flattened: by television, by moving away, by being told to “speak properly.”

This game celebrates them. Every clip is a real person talking in their own voice: mayors and fishermen, coaches and union leaders, neighbors telling stories about their block, people reading the same short paragraph as hundreds of others. Nobody here is doing an impression.

There’s no right way to sound. A Pittsburgh “yinz,” a Boston “wicked,” a Cajun lilt or a Texas drawl each carries a little history: who settled there, who worked there, who stayed.

Listen closely, guess where they’re from, and notice how much a voice tells you before it says anything about itself.
