MC Guesser

Can you tell who's rapping from the sound of their voice? Five MCs a game, ten pads each, every pad an 8-second verse from the same MC.

Scoring

Each round has a pot of 20. The pads are priced by how easy they are: pad 1 is the hardest clip and free; then 1, 1, 2, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 3. Buy all ten and there's nothing left. Once you've paid for a pad you can replay it for free.

Rounds 1 and 2 give you four names to choose from (same era, and the same voice register, so the choices don't give it away). Rounds 3 to 5 you type the name yourself.

One guess per round. Right, and you bank what's left in the pot. Wrong, or a pass, and you get zero. Five rounds, 100 points.

The clips

Hardest and easiest are set by how many plays each song has on YouTube: the free pad is the deepest cut, the dearest pad is the biggest hit. Every clip starts on a verse line (taken from time-synced lyrics), never on the hook, and the lines on the clip don't say the MC's name or the song title.

Songs are 1980–2005, mostly the MC's own records, all streamed from YouTube, mostly from the artist's official or "Topic" channel. Where an MC raps in a group, it's a group where they're the only (or near-only) voice.
